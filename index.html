What's the job?

What people ask before we climb up

Do you do insurance work?

Yes. We photograph the damage, write a clear report and a quote you can send to your insurer, and make the roof watertight while they decide.

Can you repair rather than replace?

Usually, yes. Most roofs need a repair, not a new roof. We'll tell you honestly which yours needs, with photos from up there so you can see for yourself.

How long does a re-roof take?

A typical three-bed house takes five to eight working days, depending on the roof and the weather. It's sheeted and watertight every night.

Do you guarantee your work?

Yes. On a real site, this answer shows the firm's actual guarantee and scheme details so customers can check them. This firm is made up, so it says CompetentRoofer registered (example).

Do you charge for quotes?

No. Quotes are free within our area, in writing, usually within two days of the visit.
